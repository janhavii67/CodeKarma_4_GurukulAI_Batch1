# Biorad Medisys: Corporate Profile, Facilities, Global Footprint, and Regulatory Governance

## 1. Corporate Profile & Executive Overview

**Biorad Medisys Private Limited** (incorporated in 2000) is one of India's leading indigenous medical technology manufacturers, specializing in high-precision, implantable medical devices, single-use surgical accessories, and minimally invasive intervention systems.

* **Corporate Mission**: To provide world-class, clinically superior, and affordable medical implants and interventional devices, placing India at the forefront of global biomedical manufacturing.
* **Core Philosophy**: Eliminating dependency on expensive imported medical implants by designing, testing, and manufacturing indigenous devices that match or exceed international performance, safety, and longevity benchmarks.
* **Leadership**: Founded and guided by experienced biomedical and healthcare leadership, including key executive direction by Suchitra Jitendra Hegde and Jitendra Hegde, in partnership with multidisciplinary medical advisory boards of orthopedic surgeons, endourologists, and neuro-interventional radiologists.
* **Operating Divisions**:
  1. **Orthovasive**: Joint arthroplasty (knee and hip replacements), sports medicine, and reconstructive implants (*"Freedom to Move"*).
  2. **Indovasive**: Endourology and gastrointestinal endoscopic access, stone management, and drainage devices (*"Precision that Empowers"*).
  3. **Neurovasive**: Neurovascular stroke intervention, flow diverters, intracranial aneurysm management, and peripheral vascular catheters (*"Extension of Life"*).

## 2. Infrastructure, Manufacturing Plants & R&D Centers

Biorad Medisys maintains advanced manufacturing and research facilities across western and southern India:

### A. Pune Corporate Campus & Advanced Fabrication Hub (Maharashtra)
* **Location**: Pashan-Sus Road / Baner Corridor, Pune, Maharashtra.
* **Scope**: Executive administration, international regulatory affairs, product development, cleanroom packaging, and high-precision CNC Swiss machining for orthopedic implant components.
* **Equipment**: Multi-axis Swiss CNC turning centers (Citizen, Star Japan), 5-axis vertical machining centers (DMG Mori Germany), Wire EDM (Electrical Discharge Machining), and robotic automated buffing and polishing systems.

### B. Bengaluru Campus & Corporate Office (Karnataka)
* **Location**: *Biorad House*, Bengaluru, Karnataka.
* **Scope**: Southern zonal management, software-hardware medical device integration, biomedical customer support, and clinical education workshops.

### C. Vemgal Industrial Area Manufacturing Facility (Kolar, Karnataka)
* **Location**: KIADB Industrial Area, Vemgal, Kolar District, Karnataka.
* **Scope**: Massive scale automated manufacturing plant dedicated to Indovasive endourological disposables, extrusion lines for medical-grade polyurethanes, braiding lines for stents and catheters, cleanroom packaging, and on-site sterilization processing.
* **Cleanrooms**: Over 20,000 sq. ft. of certified ISO Class 7 (Class 10,000) cleanrooms for catheter assembly and ISO Class 5 (Class 100) laminar flow workstations for critical sterile packaging.

### D. Aerospace & Hi-Tech R&D Center (Devanahalli, Bengaluru)
* **Location**: Karnataka Industrial Areas Development Board (KIADB) Aerospace, Defence & Hi-Tech Park, Devanahalli, Bengaluru.
* **Scope**: Advanced biomaterial research, additive manufacturing (3D printing of trabecular titanium matrices), finite element analysis (FEA), biomechanical wear simulation, and neurovascular flow diverter micro-braiding engineering.

## 4. Global Market Footprint & Clinical Network

* **Global Export Reach**: Exporting to over 40 countries across Western & Eastern Europe, the Middle East (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Egypt), Southeast Asia (Vietnam, Philippines, Indonesia, Malaysia), Africa (Kenya, Nigeria, South Africa), and Latin America (Brazil, Colombia).
* **Clinical Institutional Partnerships**: Installed in and utilized by premier tertiary healthcare hospital chains, medical colleges, and specialty surgery centers, including AIIMS (All India Institute of Medical Sciences), Apollo Hospitals, Fortis Healthcare, Manipal Hospitals, and Max Healthcare.
* **Clinical Education & Gurukul Simulation**: Conducts hands-on surgical training workshops, wet-lab cadaveric masterclasses, and digital simulation programs for resident surgeons, surgical fellows, and OR scrub nurses.
