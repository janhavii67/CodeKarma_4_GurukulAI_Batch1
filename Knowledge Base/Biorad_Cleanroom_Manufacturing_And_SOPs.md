# Biorad Medisys: Cleanroom Manufacturing Protocols, Machining & SOPs

## 1. Cleanroom Architecture and Environmental Monitoring
Biorad Medisys operates advanced cleanroom suites designed in accordance with **ISO 14644-1** and **WHO-GMP Class C and Class B** standards for medical implant and sterile catheter manufacturing.

### Cleanroom Classification
- **Primary Assembly & Packaging Areas:** **ISO Class 7** (Class 10,000) cleanroom suites with minimum 30 air changes per hour (ACH).
- **Critical Packaging Zones & Sterile Sealing:** **ISO Class 5** (Class 100) laminar flow workstations with HEPA filtration (efficiency 99.997% at 0.3 micron).
- **Pressure Cascade:** Positive differential pressure of 10 to 15 Pascals (Pa) maintained between cleanroom suites and external corridors to prevent particulate ingress.
- **Environmental Parameters:**
  - Temperature: 20°C ± 2°C.
  - Relative Humidity (RH): 45% ± 5% RH.
  - Airborne Particulate Limits (ISO 7): <= 352,000 particles/m³ at >= 0.5 µm; <= 2,930 particles/m³ at >= 5.0 µm.

## 2. Gowning and Hygiene Standard Operating Procedure (SOP-CR-012)

### Airlock Entry Protocol
1. **Shoe Transition:** Remove street footwear at the designated step-over bench; step into dedicated primary cleanroom clogs.
2. **Hand Hygiene:** Wash hands with anti-microbial soap for minimum 30 seconds; dry with lint-free hot air dryer. Disinfect with 70% sterile Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA).
3. **Primary Gowning:** Don lint-free disposable hair net (covering all hair) and non-shedding surgical mask (molded closely over nose and mouth).
4. **Coverall Donning:** Put on continuous non-woven polyester cleanroom jumpsuit without letting sleeves or legs contact the floor.
5. **Booties & Double Gloving:** Don high-top cleanroom booties with snap closures over trouser legs. Don sterile powder-free nitrile gloves; pull glove cuffs over jumpsuit sleeves.
6. **Air Shower:** Pass through the 15-second multi-directional high-velocity air shower chamber (nozzle velocity > 25 m/s) before entering the ISO 7 assembly zone.

## 3. Precision Machining and Surface Treatment SOPs

### 5-Axis CNC Milling of Knee Implants (SOP-MCH-04)
- **Material:** Medical-grade CoCrMo (ASTM F75) or Titanium Alloy Ti6Al4V (ASTM F136).
- **Cutting Fluids:** Fully synthetic, chlorine-free, biocompatible water-miscible coolants validated for medical implants.
- **Tooling:** Diamond-coated and solid carbide end mills with continuous tool-wear laser monitoring.
- **Tolerances:** Dimensional tolerances held within **±0.005 mm (5 microns)**; surface roughness prior to polishing target Ra <= 0.4 microns.

### Multi-Stage Ultrasonic Cleaning & Passivation (SOP-CLN-08)
1. **Pre-Wash:** High-pressure spray de-greasing in neutral aqueous surfactant bath at 55°C.
2. **Ultrasonic Stage 1:** 40 kHz multi-transducer cavitation bath in alkaline cleaner at 60°C for 15 minutes to dislodge micro-particulates and machine oils.
3. **Ultrasonic Stage 2:** 80 kHz cavitation rinse in deionized water (conductivity < 1.0 µS/cm).
4. **Passivation (ASTM A967 / ASTM F86):** Nitric acid (20-25% v/v) or high-grade citric acid bath at 50°C for 30 minutes to form a continuous chromium-oxide passive protective barrier.
5. **Final RO/DI Cascade Rinse:** Triple-cascade reverse osmosis deionized water rinse ensuring zero chemical residues (conductivity < 0.1 µS/cm).
6. **HEPA Hot-Air Drying:** Clean-air oven drying at 70°C in Class 100 HEPA environment.

## 4. Packaging, Heat Sealing, and Sterilization Validation

### Sterile Barrier Packaging (ISO 11607)
- **Primary Package:** Medical-grade Tyvek (DuPont Tyvek 1073B) sealed to thermoformed PETG (Polyethylene Terephthalate Glycol) blister tray.
- **Sealing Validation:** Rotary/impulse heat sealers operating at qualified process window:
  - Temperature: 125°C ± 3°C.
  - Pressure: 4.2 bar ± 0.2 bar.
  - Dwell Time: 2.5 seconds ± 0.2 seconds.
- **Integrity Testing:**
  - Tensile Seal Strength: Peel test according to ASTM F88 (minimum 1.5 N/15mm).
  - Dye Penetration: Leak test according to ASTM F1929.
  - Bubble Emission: Internal pressure leak test according to ASTM F2096.

### Sterilization Modalities
- **Ethylene Oxide (EtO) Sterilization (ISO 11135):** Used for polymer disposables and catheters (Indovasive Double J stents). Cycle validated for Sterility Assurance Level (SAL) 10⁻⁶ with degassing aeration to reach ethylene oxide residue < 4.0 mg/device.
- **Gamma Irradiation (ISO 11137):** Used for orthopedic joint implants (Orthovasive Excel MPK). Minimum dose 25 kGy (Cobalt-60 source) achieving SAL 10⁻⁶ without polymer degradation.
