# Biorad Orthovasive: Complete Arthroplasty, Revision & Sports Medicine Catalog

## 1. Primary Total Knee Arthroplasty (TKA) Systems

### A. GENUIN Total Knee System (GENUIN PS & GENUIN CR)
- **Regulatory Clearance:** US FDA 510(k) cleared under **K200765** (Class II, 21 CFR 888.3560).
- **Kinematic Concept:** Single-Radius (SR) femoral sagittal geometry designed to maintain constant collateral ligament isometry throughout 0° to 155° of flexion, eliminating mid-flexion ligament laxity and joint instability.
- **Deep Flexion Architecture (Hiflex):** Engineered for up to **155° of safe flexion** to accommodate deep squatting, kneeling, and cross-legged sitting.
- **Tibial Tray & Locking System:**
  - Highly polished Cobalt-Chromium-Molybdenum (CoCrMo) tibial baseplate.
  - Dovetail perimeter locking mechanism combined with an anterior locking wire ensures high pull-out resistance and virtually eliminates micro-motion backside polyethylene wear.
- **Sizing Matrix:**
  - 8 Femoral component sizes (anatomical left and right).
  - 7 Tibial baseplate sizes.
  - Articular inserts in multiple thickness options (9 mm, 11 mm, 13 mm, 15 mm, 18 mm) in both Posterior-Stabilized (PS) and Cruciate-Retaining (CR) configurations.

### B. EXCEL Medial Pivot Knee (EXCEL MPK)
- **Natural Knee Kinematics:** Replicates the asymmetric motion of the healthy human knee:
  - **Medial Compartment:** Deep spherical dish acting as a ball-and-socket pivot point with < 1 mm anteroposterior translation.
  - **Lateral Compartment:** Open, flat contoured articular track permitting unrestricted lateral femoral rollback (up to 15 mm) and physiological external tibial rotation.
- **Elimination of Paradoxical Slide:** Unlike traditional symmetric knees that slide forward during flexion, EXCEL MPK maintains posterior rollback, providing high quadriceps mechanical advantage, effortless stair climbing, and natural feel.

### C. OURO EXCEL MPK (TiNbN Gold Surface Coating)
- **Coating Technology:** Physical Vapor Deposition (PVD) Titanium Niobium Nitride (**TiNbN**) ceramic layer.
- **Clinical Indication:** Specifically designed for patients with confirmed or suspected metal hypersensitivity (allergies to Nickel, Cobalt, or Chromium).
- **Technical Metrics:**
  - Coating Thickness: 3.5 to 5.0 µm.
  - Adhesion Bond Strength: > 70 MPa (exceeds ASTM requirements).
  - Surface Hardness: ~2000 HV (Vickers), more than twice that of standard CoCrMo alloy (~800 HV).
  - Ion Elution Suppression: Reduces Nickel, Cobalt, and Chromium release into synovial tissue by **> 99%**.
  - Tribological Superiority: Decreases Ultra-High-Molecular-Weight Polyethylene (UHMWPE) articular wear by up to **65%**, dramatically lowering osteolysis risk.

## 2. Complex Revision & Realignment Knee Solutions

### A. EVOCON Revision Knee System
- **Indications:** Severe ligament instability, significant bone loss, joint arthrofibrosis, or failure of prior primary TKA.
- **Modularity:**
  - Constrained Condylar Knee (CCK) tibial inserts with tall, reinforced central spine.
  - Modular femoral and tibial intramedullary extension stems (straight and offset stems in lengths from 50 mm to 150 mm).
  - Modular distal and posterior femoral augments, as well as tibial hemi-wedges and full block augments (5 mm, 10 mm, 15 mm) for joint line reconstruction.

### B. High Tibial Osteotomy (HTO) Plate System
- **Indications:** Medial compartment osteoarthritis in active, younger patients with varus deformity.
- **Features:** Anatomically pre-contoured titanium locking plates for medial opening wedge osteotomies, allowing early weight-bearing and preservation of the biological knee joint.

## 3. Total Hip Arthroplasty (THA) Solutions

### A. Femoral Stems
- **Cementless Stems:** Tapered wedge titanium alloy (Ti6Al4V) stems with proximal porous titanium plasma spray and 50 µm Hydroxyapatite (HA) coating (ASTM F1185) promoting rapid biological osseointegration.
- **Cemented Stems:** Double-tapered polished high-nitrogen stainless steel or CoCrMo stems designed according to the French/Exeter taper-slip philosophy.

### B. Acetabular Systems & Bearings
- **Cementless Acetabular Cups:** Hemispherical titanium shell with multi-hole cluster configurations, equatorial press-fit macro-geometry, and titanium plasma/HA outer surface.
- **Articular Liners:** Highly Cross-Linked Polyethylene (XLPE) with Vitamin E antioxidant stabilization, minimizing free radicals and eliminating wear debris oxidation.
- **Modular Femoral Heads:** 28 mm, 32 mm, and 36 mm diameters available in high-purity Cobalt-Chrome and Ceramic BIOLOX Delta.

## 4. Sports Medicine Portfolio

- **ACL/PCL Reconstruction:**
  - **Suspensory Cortical Fixation:** Adjustable-loop titanium cortical buttons for femoral graft fixation.
  - **Interference Screws:** Available in bioabsorbable PLLA/HA (70% Poly-L-lactic acid / 30% Hydroxyapatite) and non-absorbable PEEK-OPTIMA with rounded thread profiles to protect soft tissue grafts.
- **Meniscal Repair:** All-Inside meniscal repair devices featuring low-profile PEEK implants, pre-tied sliding knots, and ultra-high molecular weight polyethylene (UHMWPE) braided sutures.
- **Shoulder & Soft Tissue Fixation:** Knotless PEEK suture anchors and titanium suture anchors for Bankart, SLAP, and rotator cuff repairs.
