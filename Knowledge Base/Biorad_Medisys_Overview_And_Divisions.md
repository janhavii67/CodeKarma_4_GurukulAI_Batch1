# Biorad Medisys: Corporate Overview, Divisions, and Quality Systems

## 1. Corporate Profile & Manufacturing Heritage
Biorad Medisys Pvt. Ltd. is one of India's premier medical technology innovators and medical device manufacturers, dedicated to advancing human mobility and healthcare outcomes worldwide. With advanced manufacturing plants and R&D centers located in Bangalore and Pune, India, Biorad Medisys exports precision medical implants, interventional hardware, and surgical disposables to over 50 countries across Europe, Asia-Pacific, the Middle East, and Latin America.

### Vision & Commitment
- **Core Mission:** "Healing Humanity, Powered by Science" — delivering accessible, world-class medical innovations with uncompromised precision and patient-centric design.
- **Manufacturing Footprint:** State-of-the-art facilities equipped with Class 10,000 (ISO Class 7) and Class 100 (ISO Class 5) cleanrooms, multi-axis CNC Swiss-machining centers, advanced metallurgical laboratories, and in-house sterilization validation.

## 2. The Three Foundational Pillars

Biorad Medisys organizes its comprehensive medical portfolio across three specialized clinical divisions:

### Pillar 1: Orthovasive (Orthopedics & Sports Medicine)
- **Clinical Scope:** Arthroplasty (joint replacement), trauma fixation, spine preservation, and sports medicine.
- **Flagship Solutions:**
  - **Excel MPK & Ouro Excel MPK:** Advanced Medial Pivot Knee replacement systems designed for natural kinematics and high flexion.
  - **Indus & Genuin Knee Systems:** Cruciate-retaining (CR) and posterior-stabilized (PS) total knee arthroplasty implants.
  - **Hip Systems:** Cementless porous-coated stems, cemented polished stems, modular acetabular systems, and bipolar hip prostheses.
  - **Sports Medicine:** Bio-absorbable and PEEK interference screws, titanium suture anchors, and meniscal repair implants.

### Pillar 2: Indovasive (Endoscopic & Minimally Invasive Devices)
- **Clinical Scope:** Endourology, Gastroenterology, Interventional Radiology, Gynecology, and Vascular Access.
- **Flagship Solutions:**
  - **Urology Drainage:** Double 'J' Stent series including *Releez* (short-term), *Bio Flex* (mid-term), and *Pretoflex* (long-term up to 12 months).
  - **Stone Management:** Nitinol tipless and helical stone baskets, ureteral access sheaths, hydrophilic guidewires, and dilation balloon catheters.
  - **Endoscopic Accessories:** TURP loop electrodes, biopsy forceps, sclerotherapy needles, and biliary drainage catheters.

### Pillar 3: Neurovasive (Neurovascular & Peripheral Interventions)
- **Clinical Scope:** Stroke treatment, ischemic and hemorrhagic neurovascular interventions, and peripheral arterial disease (PAD).
- **Flagship Solutions:**
  - **Flow Diverters:** Braided micro-mesh implants for intracranial aneurysm management.
  - **Revascularization Systems:** Mechanical thrombectomy devices and aspiration catheters for acute ischemic stroke.
  - **PTA Balloon Catheters:** High-pressure peripheral transluminal angioplasty balloons for peripheral vascular disease.

## 3. Quality Accreditations and Regulatory Standards

### International Certifications
- **ISO 13485:2016:** Certified Quality Management System specific to medical devices, ensuring strict design controls, supplier verification, and traceability.
- **ISO 9001:2015:** Quality management certification across all corporate and operational processes.
- **CE Marking (EU Conformity):** Conformance to European Medical Device Regulations (MDR 2017/745), enabling widespread clinical use across European healthcare networks.
- **WHO-GMP (Good Manufacturing Practices):** Adherence to World Health Organization manufacturing standards for sterile medical devices.

### Quality Control Infrastructure
- **Dimensional Metrology:** Coordinate Measuring Machines (CMM), optical vision measuring systems (tolerance precision down to ±2 microns).
- **Biomechanical Testing:** Fatigue testing rigs, cyclic loading simulators (testing joint implants beyond 5 million cycles according to ASTM F1801 and ISO 14243).
- **Metallurgical & Polymer Labs:** Differential Scanning Calorimetry (DSC), Fourier Transform Infrared (FTIR) spectroscopy, and inductively coupled plasma (ICP) testing.
