## 2. Double 'J' Stent Technology & Material Science

Ureteral stents are temporary or permanent conduits placed between the renal pelvis and the urinary bladder to bypass obstructions, relieve hydronephrosis, and promote healing following endourological procedures (URS, PCNL, RIRS, and lithotripsy).

### Indovasive Stent Portfolio

#### A. Releez Stent (Short-Term Indwelling)
- **Indication:** Routine post-ureteroscopy drainage, lithotripsy recovery, and acute ureteral edema.
- **Material:** Medical-grade thermoplastic polyurethane (TPU).
- **Indwelling Duration:** Recommended for up to 30 days.
- **Features:** Hydrophilic coating on outer surface facilitates smooth navigation over guidewires with reduced mucosal irritation; tapered tip for easy orifice entry.

#### B. Bio Flex Stent (Mid-Term Indwelling)
- **Indication:** Sub-acute ureteral strictures, post-reconstructive surgery, and prolonged ureteral drainage.
- **Material:** Proprietary biocompatible polymer blend providing optimal balance between radial kink resistance and column flexibility.
- **Indwelling Duration:** Up to 90 days (3 months).
- **Features:** Soft bladder and renal pigtails (coils) to reduce bladder trigone irritation, flank pain, and reflux; high radiopacity under fluoroscopy.

#### C. Pretoflex Stent (Long-Term Indwelling)
- **Indication:** Malignant ureteral compression (extrinsic tumors), chronic strictures, retroperitoneal fibrosis, and patients requiring prolonged drainage without frequent exchanges.
- **Material:** Advanced Carbothane / aliphatic polycarbonate polyurethane copolymer.
- **Indwelling Duration:** Validated for up to **365 days (12 months)**.
- **Features:** Superior encrustation resistance against calcium oxalate and struvite crystal deposition; maintains high tensile strength and lumen patency in acidic and alkaline urine environments.

## 3. Endourological Intervention Hardware

### A. Ureteral Access Sheaths (UAS)
- **Construction:** Dual-layer extrusion with high-tensile stainless steel or nitinol flat wire braiding to prevent ovalization and kinking during tortuous anatomy passage.
- **Coating:** Continuous hydrophilic lubrication coating on the sheath shaft.
- **Clinical Benefit:** Enables repeated flexible ureteroscope insertions without damaging the ureteral wall, while maintaining continuous low intrarenal pressure irrigation.

### B. Nitinol Stone Retrieval Baskets
- **Configurations:** Tipless 4-wire baskets, helical 4-wire and 6-wire designs, and multi-wire flat nitinol configurations.
- **Nitinol Superelasticity:** Shape-memory alloy retains opening diameter and elasticity even after repeated mechanical stone trapping and release.
- **Clinical Advantage:** Tipless design permits extraction of calyceal stones directly at the base of the lower pole without tissue puncture risk.

### C. Guidewires and Dilation Sets
- **Hydrophilic Guidewires (Zebra & Glidewire types):** Nitinol core with tungsten-loaded polyurethane jacket and cross-linked hydrophilic surface coating.
- **Amplatz Dilation Sets:** Graduated radiopaque dilators and working sheaths for establishing percutaneous renal access tracks during PCNL.
- **High-Pressure Balloon Dilators:** Multi-layered non-compliant balloon material validated up to 30 atmospheres (atm) burst pressure for dilating dense ureteral strictures.
