### A. Olympia Mechanical Aspiration System (Stroke Thrombectomy)

The Olympia Aspiration System is a dedicated, high-vacuum mechanical aspiration platform specifically optimized for rapid, atraumatic clot retrieval during emergent acute ischemic stroke interventions (Large Vessel Occlusions - LVO).

#### 1. Olympia High-Vacuum Pump Specifications
* **Vacuum Performance**: Generates deep continuous negative pressure up to **-27.5 in/Hg (-93 kPa / 700 mmHg)** within less than **3 seconds** of activation.
* **Flow Rate**: High displacement volumetric airflow (>32 L/min) ensuring instantaneous transmission of suction force to the catheter tip upon contacting the thrombus.
* **Ergonomics & Control**: Streamlined single-button push control, portable medical cart with silent rolling casters, and dual pressure gauges (preset and real-time vacuum).
* **Fluid Management**: Self-locking 1000 mL and 2000 mL suction canisters fitted with:
  * Integrated fine-mesh **Clot Catcher / Filter Basket** for pathological clot histology.
  * Dual hydrophobic **0.2 µm Antimicrobial / Antiviral Filters** preventing aerosolized bio-contaminants from reaching pump internals.
* **Tubing**: Medical-grade, reinforced, non-collapsible vacuum tubing ensuring continuous flow without lumen collapse.

#### 2. Large-Bore Aspiration Catheter Portfolio
* **Olympia 071 Aspiration Catheter**:
  * Inner Diameter (ID): **0.071" (1.80 mm)**—maximizes cross-sectional thrombus ingestion area.
  * Outer Diameter (OD): **6.0 Fr (2.05 mm)** distal profile.
  * Lengths: **125 cm and 132 cm**.
* **Olympia 068 Aspiration Catheter**:
  * Inner Diameter (ID): **0.068" (1.73 mm)**.
  * Lengths: **128 cm and 135 cm**.
* **Shaft Architecture**:
  * 16-zone progressive transition shaft featuring high-torque proximal stainless steel braiding for 1:1 tactile pushability and a distal superelastic Nitinol helical coil zone for extreme flexibility.
  * Zero ovalization across the acute bends of the carotid siphon (ophthalmic and cavernous segments).
  * Distal 25 cm coated with durable cross-linked hydrophilic polymer (HydroGlide™) for friction-free navigation into the Middle Cerebral Artery (M1/M2 segments).

### B. Neurovasive Intracranial Flow Diverter

The Neurovasive Flow Diverter is a self-expanding, micro-braided tubular endoprosthesis designed to treat wide-necked, fusiform, and giant intracranial aneurysms of the internal carotid artery (ICA) and vertebrobasilar circulation.

* **Dual-Wire Braid Technology**:
  * Precision-braided using a synergistic combination of **Drawn Filled Tube (DFT) platinum-cored Nitinol wires** and high-tensile **Cobalt-Chromium-Molybdenum wires**.
  * Provides superior radiopacity across the entire stent length without requiring bulky distal/proximal radiopaque markers.
* **Distinctive Checkerboard Pattern**:
  * Delivers a calculated surface metal coverage of **60% to 70%** over the aneurysm neck.
  * Disrupts intra-aneurysmal vortex flow, inducing stagnant thrombosis and biological endothelial neointimal growth across the neck, while simultaneously maintaining physiological laminar blood flow into side branch vessels (such as the ophthalmic artery or anterior choroidal artery).
* **Kink Resistance & Radial Force**: High hoop strength ensures complete apposition to tortuous arterial walls without ovalization or flaring.
* **Delivery System & Resheathability**:
  * Delivered through 0.021" or 0.027" neuro microcatheters.
  * Core wire with low-friction micro-polymer bumps and deployment coils allows controlled delivery, recapture, and **resheathing up to 90% of the deployed length** for micro-adjustment of placement prior to final detachment.
* **Sizes**: Diameters **2.5 mm to 5.0 mm** (in 0.5 mm steps); lengths **10 mm to 35 mm**.

## 2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for Neurovascular Interventions

### SOP-NEURO-01: Direct Aspiration Thrombectomy (ADAPT) for Acute Ischemic Stroke
1. **Patient Preparation & Access**: Patient with acute ischemic stroke secondary to Large Vessel Occlusion (LVO) of ICA terminus or MCA M1 segment within window. Gain transfemoral or transradial access using an 8 Fr vascular sheath.
2. **Guide Catheter Positioning**: Advance an 8 Fr balloon guide catheter or 6 Fr long guide sheath into the high cervical internal carotid artery under fluoroscopy.
3. **Catheter Tri-Axial Assembly**: Assemble Olympia 071 Aspiration Catheter over a 0.021" microcatheter and 0.014" neuro microwire.
4. **Intracranial Navigation**: Under continuous roadmap fluoroscopy, advance microwire and microcatheter past the ophthalmic segment into the proximal face of the thrombus. Track the Olympia 071 catheter over the microcatheter until the catheter tip contacts the proximal margin of the clot.
5. **Microcatheter Removal**: Withdraw the microwire and microcatheter, leaving the Olympia 071 lumen completely unobstructed.
6. **Aspiration Connection**: Connect the proximal Luer lock of the Olympia 071 catheter to the kink-resistant tubing of the Olympia High-Vacuum Pump via a high-flow stopcock.
7. **Vacuum Activation**: Switch the Olympia pump ON (-27.5 in/Hg). Open the stopcock.
8. **Thrombus Engagement**:
   * Observe fluid movement in the aspiration canister.
   * Flow will abruptly halt when the thrombus seals against the catheter tip (vacuum gauge holds at -27.5 in/Hg).
   * Maintain continuous suction for **90 seconds** to allow thrombus to mold and wedge firmly inside the catheter tip.
9. **Catheter Extraction**: Slowly withdraw the Olympia 071 catheter under continuous negative suction into the guide catheter.
10. **Reperfusion Verification**: Confirm captured thrombus inside the canister clot filter. Perform diagnostic angiography through guide catheter to verify TICI 2b/3 (Thrombolysis in Cerebral Infarction) full arterial recanalization.

### SOP-NEURO-02: Endovascular Flow Diverter Deployment for Wide-Neck Aneurysms
1. **Parent Vessel Sizing**: Perform 3D rotational catheter angiography. Measure parent artery diameter at the proximal landing zone, aneurysm neck, and distal landing zone. Select flow diverter diameter matching the proximal landing zone diameter (or +0.25 mm for press-fit apposition).
2. **Microcatheter Placement**: Navigate 0.027" microcatheter past the aneurysm neck into the distal parent artery (landing zone minimum 5 mm distal to neck).
3. **Device Introduction**: Flush flow diverter delivery sheath with heparinized saline. Introduce flow diverter into microcatheter hub. Advance delivery wire until device reaches microcatheter tip.
4. **Anchor & Unsheath**: Stabilize delivery wire; slowly withdraw microcatheter to uncover the distal 3–5 mm of the flow diverter. Confirm radial expansion and wall apposition under high-magnification fluoroscopy.
5. **Push-Pull Deployment**: While gradually withdrawing the microcatheter, gently advance the delivery wire to "push" the mesh, expanding braid density across the aneurysm neck.
6. **Resheathing Assessment**: If wall apposition is suboptimal or positioning needs adjustment, re-advance microcatheter to resheath device (up to 90% length) and reposition.
7. **Final Release**: Once fully expanded and wall apposition is confirmed across neck, actuate detachment mechanism. Withdraw delivery core wire and microcatheter. Perform control angiography: confirm contrast stasis inside aneurysm dome and preservation of adjacent branch arteries.
