# Biorad Orthovasive: Knee and Hip Arthroplasty Engineering Specifications

## 1. The Kinematic Concept: Medial Pivot Knee (MPK) Architecture
Traditional total knee arthroplasty (TKA) designs often suffer from "paradoxical anterior slide" during flexion, where the femur slides forward unnaturally on the tibial plateau, causing mid-flexion instability, clicking sounds, quadriceps inefficiency, and patient dissatisfaction.

### The Natural Knee Kinematic Model
Natural human knee kinematics demonstrate asymmetric movement during flexion:
- **Medial Compartment:** Functions primarily as a ball-and-socket pivot point. It exhibits high conformity, minimal anterior-posterior (AP) translation (typically < 1 mm), and superior load transfer throughout the gait cycle.
- **Lateral Compartment:** Exhibits controlled posterior rollback (up to 10-15 mm during deep flexion) and rotation around the medial pivot center, enabling smooth, natural knee bending.

## 2. Excel MPK System Specifications

### Key Design Innovations
1. **Spherical Medial Geometry:**
   - Highly congruent medial femoral condyle and tibial articular insert create constant contact area across 0° to 155° of flexion.
   - Eliminates anterior sliding and maintains joint stability throughout stairs descent, squatting, and walking.
2. **Open Lateral Geometry:**
   - Flat lateral tibial track allows unrestricted physiological lateral femoral rollback and axial rotation.
3. **Deep Flexion Capability:**
   - Engineered and clearance-optimized to safely achieve up to **155° flexion** without patellofemoral impingement or edge loading.
   - Specifically accommodates cultural activities requiring deep kneeling, cross-legged sitting, and active sports.
4. **Bone Preservation:**
   - Optimized anterior flange contour minimizes patellar crepitus and preserves anterior femoral bone stock.

## 3. Ouro Excel MPK: Advanced TiNbN Surface Engineering

### The Metal Allergy Challenge
Standard orthopedic implants utilize Cobalt-Chromium-Molybdenum (CoCrMo) alloys. Trace ion release (nickel, cobalt, chromium) can trigger type IV delayed hypersensitivity reactions in sensitized patients, leading to chronic joint effusion, aseptic loosening, pain, and early revision surgery.

### Ouro Excel MPK Technical Solution
- **Coating Technology:** Physical Vapor Deposition (PVD) applied Titanium Niobium Nitride (**TiNbN**) ceramic coating.
- **Physical Characteristics:**
  - Distinctive radiant golden finish.
  - Coating thickness: 3.5 to 5.0 microns with high adhesive bonding strength (> 70 MPa).
  - Ceramic hardness: ~2000 Vickers Pyramid Number (HV), more than double the hardness of uncoated CoCr alloys.
- **Key Clinical Benefits:**
  - **Complete Ion Barrier:** Prevents nickel and cobalt ion elution into synovial fluid by >99%.
  - **Superior Tribology:** Extreme surface smoothness (Ra < 0.01 microns) significantly reduces wear on the UHMWPE (Ultra-High-Molecular-Weight Polyethylene) tibial insert by up to 65%.
  - **Wettability:** Highly hydrophilic surface promotes continuous synovial fluid film lubrication, reducing friction and extending implant longevity.

## 4. Complementary Orthopedic Solutions

### Indus & Genuin Knee Systems
- **Cruciate-Retaining (CR):** For patients with intact posterior cruciate ligament (PCL); provides anatomical distal femoral cut and conservative bone resection.
- **Posterior-Stabilized (PS):** Features an optimized spine-and-cam mechanism ensuring controlled mechanical rollback when the PCL is compromised.

### Hip Arthroplasty Portfolio
- **Cementless Femoral Stems:** Titanium alloy (Ti6Al4V) with dual-layer plasma-sprayed titanium and hydroxyapatite (HA) coating for rapid biological osseointegration.
- **Acetabular Systems:** Hemispherical titanium shell with multi-hole cluster options and highly cross-linked polyethylene (XLPE) or ceramic articulation liners.
