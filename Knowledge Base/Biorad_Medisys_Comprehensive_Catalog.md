# Biorad Medisys: Comprehensive Enterprise & Product Encyclopedia

## 1. Corporate Identity and Global Footprint
**Biorad Medisys Pvt. Ltd.** is an internationally recognized medical device technology company headquartered in India. Built around the ethos *"Healing Humanity, Powered by Science"*, Biorad Medisys develops, engineers, and manufactures precision orthopedic implants, minimally invasive endosurgical systems, and neurovascular intervention devices.

### Strategic Infrastructure & Locations
- **Corporate Headquarters & Primary Manufacturing Facility (Pune):**
  - Location: Pashan-Sus Road, Pune, Maharashtra, India.
  - Operations: Corporate administration, design engineering, Arthroplasty (Orthovasive) machining, cleanroom packaging, and global logistics.
- **Advanced Technology & Cleanroom Manufacturing Centers (Bangalore & Kolar):**
  - Bangalore Regional Hub: Hitech Defence and Aerospace Park, Bangalore, Karnataka.
  - Kolar Facility: Vemgal Industrial Area, Kolar, Karnataka.
  - Operations: Advanced polymer extrusion, catheter braiding, Indovasive cleanroom assembly lines, and specialized R&D testing laboratories.
- **Global Reach:** Products are CE-marked, regulatory-approved, and distributed across more than **50 countries worldwide**, including healthcare networks in Europe, Asia-Pacific, Latin America, and the Middle East.

## 3. Foundational Clinical Pillars

Biorad Medisys groups its vast portfolio into three specialized medical divisions:

### 1. Orthovasive (Orthopedics & Sports Medicine)
Focuses on preserving joint mobility, anatomical restoration, and kinematic naturalness across joint replacement (TKA, THA), revision surgery, spinal stabilization, and sports medicine.
- **Key Products:** Genuin Total Knee System (FDA 510(k)), Excel MPK (Medial Pivot Knee), Ouro Excel MPK (TiNbN gold coating), Evocon Revision Knee System, HTO Plates, Cementless & Cemented Hip Systems, and Sports Medicine Anchors/Screws.

### 2. Indovasive (Endoscopic & Surgical Disposables)
Focuses on disposable and capital instrumentation for minimally invasive interventions in Endourology, Gastroenterology, Interventional Radiology, and Vascular Access.
- **Key Products:** Double 'J' Stents (*Releez*, *Bio Flex*, *Pretoflex* up to 365 days), Ureteral Access Sheaths, Tipless Nitinol Stone Baskets, Guidewires (Zebra/Hydrophilic), Amplatz & Balloon Dilation Systems, Laser Fibers (Holmium & Thulium), and Resection Electrodes (TURP/TURBT).

### 3. Neurovasive (Neuro & Peripheral Vascular Interventions)
Focuses on high-consequence vascular interventions for ischemic and hemorrhagic strokes, intracranial aneurysms, and peripheral arterial disease.
- **Key Products:** Olympia Aspiration System for acute ischemic stroke mechanical thrombectomy, Intracranial Aneurysm Flow Diverters, PTA Balloon Catheters, Peripheral Self-Expanding Stents, and Microcatheters.
