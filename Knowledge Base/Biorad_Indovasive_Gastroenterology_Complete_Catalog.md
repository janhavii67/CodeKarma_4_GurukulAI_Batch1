# Biorad Indovasive Gastroenterology: Complete Product Catalog & Clinical SOPs

## 1. Product Family & Technical Specifications

Indovasive (a specialized division of Biorad Medisys Pvt. Ltd.) manufactures an advanced portfolio of gastrointestinal (GI) endoscopy devices, non-vascular self-expanding metal stents (SEMS), balloon dilators, and hemostasis solutions.

---

### A. Self-Expanding Metal Stents (SEMS)

Indovasive SEMS are manufactured from superelastic shape-memory **Nitinol (Nickel-Titanium)** wire braided in a closed-loop or laser-cut pattern to provide optimal radial expansion force, extreme flexibility across tortuous lumens, and high resistance to fracture.

### B. Endoscopic Dilation Systems

#### 1. Xpand Multistage Balloon Dilators
* **Material**: Non-compliant, high-pressure medical **PEBAX** polymer engineered to maintain uniform cylindrical diameter across high dilation pressures (up to 10 ATM) without dog-boning.
* **Three-Stage Radial Expansion**: Each balloon expands sequentially to three distinct calibrated diameters at designated atmospheric pressures:
  * 6 mm – 7 mm – 8 mm
  * 8 mm – 9 mm – 10 mm
  * 10 mm – 11 mm – 12 mm
  * 12 mm – 13.5 mm – 15 mm
  * 15 mm – 16.5 mm – 18 mm
  * 18 mm – 19 mm – 20 mm
* **Design**: Through-The-Scope (TTS) catheter compatible with 2.8 mm and 3.2 mm working channels; 2 embedded radiopaque markers indicate balloon working length (5.5 cm / 8.0 cm).
* **Clinical Indications**: Achalasia, pyloric stenosis, anastomotic strictures, radiation-induced esophageal stenosis, and Crohn's disease strictures.

#### 2. Xpand Rigid Dilator Sets (Savary-Gilliard Type)
* **Design**: Thermosensitive radiopaque PVC dilators with graduated atraumatic tapered tips.
* **Set Composition**: Diameters **5 mm to 20 mm** in 1 mm increments.
* **Guidewire**: Supplied with calibrated 0.035" stainless steel spring-tip guidewire (length 260 cm) with centimeter depth markings.

### C. Endoscopic Hemostasis & Tissue Resection

#### 1. Disposable Hemostatic Clips (Hemoclips)
* **Mechanics**: 360° bidirectional rotatable handle for precise orientation against bleeding vessels. Reopenable and repositionable multiple times prior to final deployment lock.
* **Jaw Opening Spans**: **9 mm, 11 mm, 13 mm, and 16 mm**.
* **Clinical Applications**: Active peptic ulcer bleeding (Forrest Class Ia/Ib), post-polypectomy mucosal defect closure, Dieulafoy lesions, Mallory-Weiss tears, and endoscopic mucosal resection (EMR) site closure.

#### 2. Endoscopic Injection / Sclerotherapy Needles
* **Needle Gauges**: **21G, 23G, and 25G** with ultra-sharp bevel.
* **Projection Lengths**: 4 mm, 5 mm, 6 mm.
* **Catheter**: Kink-resistant PTFE/stainless steel sheath (outer diameter 1.8 mm / 2.3 mm, working length 180 cm and 230 cm) with ergonomic one-handed ratchet handle preventing accidental needle retraction.

#### 3. Polypectomy Snares
* **Wire Geometries**: Oval, Hexagonal, and Crescent loops.
* **Sizes**: 10 mm, 15 mm, 25 mm, 30 mm loop diameters.
* **Options**: Cold snare (thin 0.24 mm braided wire for clean cold resection of small polyps) and Hot snare (thick 0.40 mm wire for electrocautery coagulation during large sessile polyp removal).

#### 4. Multi-Band Ligator for Esophageal Varices
* **Design**: Clear transparent barrel mounted onto endoscope tip pre-loaded with **6 or 7 medical-grade latex-free elastic bands**.
* **Trigger Mechanism**: High-tensile deployment cord actuated via an ergonomic handle mounted on the biopsy port, delivering positive tactile feedback for each deployed band.

## 2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for GI Endoscopy

### SOP-GI-01: Deployment of Fully Covered Esophageal SEMS
1. **Assessment & Measurement**: Perform diagnostic upper endoscopy. Identify tumor margins and stricture length. Under fluoroscopy, place radiopaque cutaneous markers at proximal and distal stricture margins. Stent length must exceed stricture by at least 2 cm at each end (total extra margin 4 cm).
2. **Guidewire Placement**: Advance 0.035" stiff guidewire (Savary-Gilliard or Zebra wire) through stricture into stomach or duodenum under endoscopic and fluoroscopic guidance.
3. **Delivery System Advancement**: Mount Indovasive Esophageal SEMS over guidewire. Advance delivery catheter through pharynx under direct fluoroscopy until upper and lower radiopaque stent markers bracket the stricture margins symmetrically.
4. **Endoscopic Visualization**: Position endoscope alongside the delivery catheter at the proximal margin to visually monitor stent emergence.
5. **Deployment**: Release safety lock on delivery handle. Gradually withdraw outer sheath while holding inner shaft stationary. Confirm progressive radial expansion of distal flange and body.
6. **Final Release**: Complete deployment of proximal flare. Withdraw delivery catheter gently over guidewire.
7. **Confirmation**: Re-introduce endoscope to inspect proximal stent lumen (do NOT pass endoscope through newly deployed stent). Confirm patent airway, absence of perforation, and correct positioning.

### SOP-GI-02: Endoscopic Hemostatic Clipping for Bleeding Ulcers
1. **Target Identification**: Clear blood clots and gastric contents using high-volume water jet irrigation through endoscope auxiliary channel. Identify bleeding vessel (Forrest Ia arterial spurting or Ib active oozing).
2. **Clip Preparation**: Advance Indovasive Hemoclip through 2.8 mm or 3.2 mm biopsy port. Keep clip closed inside catheter until tip emerges 2 cm from endoscope distal cap.
3. **Opening & Rotation**: Open clip jaws by advancing handle slider. Rotate thumb ring to orient clip jaws perpendicular to the long axis of the bleeding vessel or ulcer base.
4. **Tissue Grasping**: Press open clip firmly against the bleeding vessel and surrounding healthy mucosa to ensure deep submucosal bite.
5. **Firing**: Squeeze handle slider firmly until tactile and audible "click" confirms permanent lock and release of the clip from the delivery wire.
6. **Inspection**: Verify immediate hemostasis. If vessel caliber is large, deploy secondary clip adjacent to initial clip for complete vascular tamponade.
