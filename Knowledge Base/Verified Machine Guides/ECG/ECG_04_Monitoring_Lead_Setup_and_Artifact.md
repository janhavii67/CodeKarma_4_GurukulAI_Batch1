# ECG Monitoring Lead Setup and Artifact Checks

## Scope

This is a general training summary for the registered ECG category, whose equipment record does not name a manufacturer or model. Follow the device's instructions and the facility's clinical protocol. It does not interpret a patient's ECG or replace a clinician.

## Before a monitoring recording

1. Confirm that the selected device mode and displayed lead labels match the monitoring setup in the local protocol.
2. Prepare the skin and apply electrodes at the anatomical locations specified by the facility and device instructions. Verify cable connections and electrode contact before treating an irregular trace as physiological.
3. Check for motion, loose electrodes, cable pull, and electrical interference when the trace contains abrupt changes or baseline disturbance. Correct the setup under the local protocol and repeat the recording when appropriate.
4. If a monitoring system is used to obtain a standard diagnostic 12-lead ECG, use the diagnostic limb-electrode locations and standard chest landmarks required by the facility protocol. Torso limb-electrode placement used for continuous monitoring is not interchangeable with standard 12-lead placement.
5. Document any changed electrode position or selected lead. Verify placement before relying on a changed waveform or comparing it with an earlier recording.

## Interpretation and equipment limits

Electrode misplacement and artifact can change the recorded waveform and may imitate clinically important patterns. Automated monitor analysis and alarm features have limits; clinical interpretation and alarm settings belong to trained staff under the facility protocol. IEC 60601-2-27 describes basic safety and essential performance requirements for ECG monitoring equipment; it is not a substitute for the exact device manual.
