# ECG monitor safety and essential performance: standard scope

## What the FDA page establishes

The FDA recognizes IEC 60601-2-27 for electrocardiographic monitoring equipment. Its listed scope is basic safety and essential performance for ECG monitoring equipment used in hospital environments, including use in ambulances or air transport under the stated standard.

## What this page does not provide

The recognition page is a standards listing, not an ECG user's guide, servicing procedure, acceptance test, clinical protocol, or list of settings. It cannot establish the configuration or performance of the specific ECG equipment registered in Gurukul AI.

## Safe use boundary

For alarms, electrical safety, service, filters, gain, speed, or configuration, consult the exact model's current manufacturer instructions, the facility's biomedical-engineering process, and the responsible clinician. Do not infer a malfunction from an audio recording of beeps or from an AI-generated sound description.
