# MRI Acoustic Noise and Hearing Protection

## Scope

This guide covers patient and staff hearing precautions for the registered MRI category. It does not identify the cause of a particular scanner sound or determine whether an MRI system is operating correctly.

## Hearing protection workflow

1. Before the scan, follow the site's MRI screening and safety workflow and the scanner manufacturer's instructions.
2. Provide the hearing protection specified for the examination and approved for the MR environment. FDA notes that changing gradient magnetic fields can produce loud knocking sounds that may harm hearing without adequate protection.
3. Make sure the patient knows how to communicate discomfort and where the approved call device is, following the site's procedure.
4. If the patient reports pain, unexpected discomfort, ringing in the ears, or loss of hearing, follow the facility's response and escalation procedure and notify the responsible MRI staff.
5. Do not remove, replace, or modify hearing protection inside the MR environment unless directed under the local safety procedure. Do not treat a familiar knocking sound by itself as proof of safe operation or a fault.

## Limits

The sound produced during imaging varies with the sequence and scanner. Audio similarity cannot establish a fault, hearing-risk level, or safe operating state. Use only the site-approved hearing-protection protocol and model-specific manufacturer instructions.
