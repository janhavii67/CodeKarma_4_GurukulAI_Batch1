# MRI pre-exam screening and risk communication

## Screening information

The FDA says patients may be asked to complete a screening questionnaire before MRI. Accurate screening helps the care team check whether implanted or external devices can safely enter the MR environment. Device-specific safety conditions must be confirmed rather than guessed.

## Staff and patient communication

Explain that MRI uses a strong magnetic field and that the care team needs accurate information about implants, devices, metal, and prior procedures. If an answer is missing or uncertain, refer it to the MR safety process. Do not treat this learning guide or a Tutor response as clearance for a person or device to enter the MRI room.

## Acoustic and other MRI risks

MRI procedures can involve loud acoustic noise and other hazards. The FDA's patient guidance should be paired with current site policy and device labeling for hearing protection, screening, and exam-specific risk management. This page does not provide a scanner sound signature or a method to diagnose equipment from sound.
