# MRI quality control: weekly checks and QMP oversight

## Responsibility

ACR says an MRI quality-control program must be documented and overseen by a qualified medical physicist or MR scientist. The program includes installation and routine performance monitoring, written test methods, technologist QC, and an annual system performance evaluation. Preventive maintenance and corrective service must be scheduled and documented by a qualified service engineer.

## Weekly technologist QC

The ACR page describes a weekly minimum frequency for technologist QC and includes checks related to table positioning, setup/scanning, and center frequency, along with phantom-based image-quality checks in its tables. The QMP/MR scientist identifies the responsible person and may choose a higher frequency. The local written procedure defines the phantom, scanner workflow, control limits, and required response.

## Out-of-limit results and safety program

If a monitored parameter falls outside its action limits, corrective action should be taken with QMP/MR-scientist support for unresolved issues. ACR also expects MRI safety policies to be written, enforced, reviewed, and documented at least annually by the MR supervising physician. An annual equipment review includes the MR safety program and system integrity.

## Limits

This is facility-level ACR guidance. It does not provide exact scanner menus, service commands, sequence settings, or a substitute for the QMP-approved procedure and manufacturer instructions for the installed MRI model.
