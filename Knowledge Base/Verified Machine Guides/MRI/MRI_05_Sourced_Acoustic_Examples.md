# Public MRI scanner acoustic examples

## What these recordings are

Song Work's catalogue lists fourteen recordings of a Siemens 3T Magnetom Prisma Fit whole-body MRI scanner at the Clinical Research Imaging Facility, Ninewells Hospital & Medical School, Dundee, Scotland. The catalogue identifies the recording date as 2020-09-08, the contributor as Siôn Parkinson, and each recording as CC0. The catalogue also lists the handheld recorder used for each clip. These are real sequence recordings, not synthesized examples.

The registered Gurukul MRI machine is identified as `MACH-MRI`; its manufacturer and clinical model are not recorded. These external recordings therefore must be treated as cross-unit learning examples. They are not calibrated recordings from the registered unit, are not a normal or abnormal acceptance baseline, and have no documented equipment-fault association. Do not use them to diagnose a fault or to establish that any scanner is safe.

## Safe educational use

Use these clips to illustrate that MRI acoustic output varies with the sequence and recording setup. They do not establish a universal “normal MRI sound.” Compare no clip with another machine to infer a fault. For patient and staff protection, follow the facility's MRI hearing-protection procedure and the manufacturer's instructions. The FDA notes that changing MRI magnetic fields can produce loud knocking sounds that may harm hearing without adequate protection.

## Sources and limitations
