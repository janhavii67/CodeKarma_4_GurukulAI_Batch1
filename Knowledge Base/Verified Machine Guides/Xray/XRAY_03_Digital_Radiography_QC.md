# Digital radiography: personnel, image management, and quality control

## Applicability

The ACR–AAPM–SIIM–SPR practice parameter covers digital projection radiography using computed-radiography or digital-radiography detectors. It addresses personnel qualifications, equipment, data and image handling, radiation safety, quality control, and quality improvement. Confirm that the installed X-ray system uses a digital detector before applying this parameter.

## Training and supervision

The parameter says personnel must have appropriate training and knowledge for the equipment and procedure. It assigns responsibilities across physicians, qualified medical physicists, radiologic technologists, and imaging informatics professionals. Clinical imaging must follow the appropriate supervising physician and qualified staff arrangements.

## Quality process

The parameter treats image acquisition, processing, transmission, and data integrity as part of quality care. It should be read with current state/federal requirements, applicable exam-specific standards, local policy, and the exact manufacturer's instructions. It is a practice parameter, not a machine service manual or a list of local exposure settings.
