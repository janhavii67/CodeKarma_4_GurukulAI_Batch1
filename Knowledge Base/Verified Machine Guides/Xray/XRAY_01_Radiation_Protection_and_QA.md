# Radiography: exposure optimization and equipment QA

## Justification and optimization

The FDA describes the imaging team as responsible for exam optimization, with the referring provider responsible for justifying the exam. The aim is the lowest radiation dose reasonably necessary for image quality adequate to the clinical task. Patient size, anatomical area, clinical indication, and equipment capabilities all matter.

## Facility controls

The FDA recommends trained staff, written protocols or technique charts designed for the clinical task and patient group, properly maintained and tested equipment, and regular QC. A facility QA program should monitor dose and investigate anomalous patterns. Diagnostic reference levels support optimization; they are not dose limits.

## No universal technique chart

The FDA notes that optimized exposures can differ between facilities and equipment while meeting the same clinical task. This guide does not provide exposure factors or patient-specific technique values. Use the site's approved protocol and exact detector/generator manufacturer's instructions, with the responsible radiologist and medical physicist.
