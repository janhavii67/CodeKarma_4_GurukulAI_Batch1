# Radiography basics: appropriate imaging and minimum exposure

## Purpose of radiography

Radiography uses x-rays to create images of internal structures. The FDA's radiography guidance emphasizes that an examination should be appropriate to the clinical question and that the number of images and exposure should be kept as low as reasonably achievable while still meeting the clinical need.

## Staff practice

The FDA advises patients and caregivers to share relevant information with the imaging team, including pregnancy possibility and prior imaging where applicable. Operators need training for the equipment and the exam. Use the local protocol and equipment instructions for positioning, exposure selection, shielding policy, repeat-image decisions, and patient communication.

## Limitations

This page provides general patient and radiation-safety information. It is not a radiographer's technique chart, generator manual, or QC procedure. Do not derive technique factors or decide image acceptability from this summary.
