# Digital Radiography Exposure Indicators and Quality Control

## Scope

This guide is for the registered X-ray category, whose equipment record does not name a manufacturer or model. It summarizes quality-control concepts; local diagnostic protocols, detector-specific targets, and action limits must come from the facility, manufacturer, and qualified medical physicist (QMP).

## Routine review

1. Follow the detector and generator manufacturer's recommended maintenance, calibration, and routine QC procedure.
2. Review the displayed exposure indicator and deviation index, where available, alongside the exam type, detector, and facility target. Digital post-processing can make image brightness look acceptable across a broad range of detector exposures, so brightness alone is not an exposure check.
3. Trend exposure indicators and rejected-image reasons through the facility's QC program. Investigate repeated deviations, artifacts, or changes after service or calibration with the responsible radiographer and QMP.
4. Use only the target exposure index, deviation-index action limits, and corrective actions established for the specific detector and clinical protocol. Do not use a generic value from another model or change exposure settings solely from an AI observation.
5. Document findings and follow the local escalation process when a result is outside its action limit or image quality is uncertain.

## Limits

AAPM reports describe physicist acceptance testing and technologist ongoing QC as complementary work. Patient-dose optimization must preserve image quality adequate for the clinical task and follow the facility QA program and applicable requirements.
