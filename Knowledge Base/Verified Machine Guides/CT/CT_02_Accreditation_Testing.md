# CT accreditation testing: annual, clinical, and phantom work

## The three parts

ACR describes three parts of a CT accreditation submission: the annual system performance evaluation summary, clinical testing, and phantom testing. A unit must pass both the clinical and phantom image-quality tests for ACR CT accreditation.

## Qualified medical physicist involvement

ACR requires a qualified medical physicist (QMP) for the annual system performance evaluation, oversight of the facility's technologist QC program, and the dosimetry portion of phantom testing. The QMP follows the current ACR CT QC manual and the evaluation requirements for the unit. ACR strongly recommends QMP support for the phantom image-quality portion as well.

## How to use this guide

This summary explains responsibilities and testing categories. It does not provide the site's submission packet, test image requirements, scanner protocol values, phantom setup, or pass/fail limits. Those details can depend on the accreditation application and the installed scanner. Use the facility's ACR testing packet, written procedures, QMP direction, and exact-model manufacturer documentation.
