# CT Daily Technologist Quality Control

## Scope

This guide summarizes the ACR technologist quality-control approach for CT. The registered machine record is a CT-Scan category without a manufacturer or model number. Use the facility's current written QC procedure and the exact scanner manufacturer's instructions. A qualified medical physicist (QMP) establishes the applicable methods and action limits.

## Daily workflow

1. Perform the facility-approved phantom acquisition before the first clinical scan of the day, or at the equivalent point for a scanner operating continuously.
2. Use the phantom, scan mode, and acquisition technique specified in the written QC procedure. The ACR manual describes recording the water CT number and image standard deviation (noise).
3. Review the QC phantom images for artifacts; an automatic check does not replace the artifact review unless the QMP has approved that substitution in writing.
4. Record the measurements, mode, date, and artifact-review result in the facility QC log. Compare results only with the scanner-specific action limits established by the QMP and manufacturer.
5. If a check is outside its action limit, an artifact is present, or the result is uncertain, follow the site's escalation procedure and contact the QMP or service engineer before clinical use. Do not invent a tolerance or adjust service settings.

## Limits

Acceptance testing, annual system performance evaluation, dosimetry, repair follow-up, and action limits require the responsible qualified professionals and local written procedures. This guide does not replace the ACR manual, scanner instructions, or the facility QC program.
