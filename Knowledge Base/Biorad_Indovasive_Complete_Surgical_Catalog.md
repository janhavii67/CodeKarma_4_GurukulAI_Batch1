# Biorad Indovasive: Complete Surgical & Endourology Product Catalog

## 1. Division Profile and Mission
**Indovasive** is Biorad Medisys' specialized division dedicated to minimally invasive surgical disposables and instruments across Endourology, Gastroenterology, Interventional Radiology, and Vascular Access. Indovasive products are engineered to provide maximum procedural safety, reduce operating room time, and improve patient recovery metrics.

## 3. Endourological Intervention Hardware

### A. Ureteral Access Sheaths (UAS)
- **Structural Construction:** Composite dual-layer tube with flat stainless steel or nitinol coil braiding embedded within the inner PTFE liner and outer polyurethane jacket.
- **Performance Characteristics:**
  - Kink-resistant: Resists pinching during sharp tortuous pelvic turns.
  - Continuous Hydrophilic Coating: Facilitates smooth passage without ureteral mucosal tearing.
  - Sizing: Diameters from 9.5/11.5 Fr to 12/14 Fr; lengths of 28 cm, 35 cm, 45 cm, and 55 cm.
- **Clinical Benefit:** Reduces intrarenal pressure during high-flow fluid irrigation, mitigating pyelovenous backflow and postoperative sepsis risks.

### B. Nitinol Stone Retrieval Baskets
- **Geometry Options:**
  - **Tipless 4-Wire Baskets:** Enables stone capture directly off the urothelium and within lower calyces without tissue perforation.
  - **Helical 4-Wire & 6-Wire Baskets:** Creates an enveloping spiral cage to entrap fragmented stones and calculi during URS and PCNL.
- **Material Science:** High-elasticity shape-memory nitinol (NiTi) wire that resists permanent deformation after forceful stone traction.

### C. Guidewires and Dilation Instruments
- **Zebra & Hydrophilic Nitinol Guidewires:** Kink-resistant superelastic nitinol core with radiopaque tungsten-polyurethane jacket, coated with cross-linked hydrophilic polymer. Features striped (Zebra) outer jacket for fluoroscopic and endoscopic motion tracking.
- **Amplatz Extra-Stiff Guidewires:** PTFE-coated stainless steel core providing high column stability for rail-tracking heavy access instruments into the kidney.
- **High-Pressure Balloon Dilators:** Dual-lumen non-compliant balloon catheter rated up to **30 atm burst pressure**, designed for dilating tight ureteral strictures and establishing PCNL nephrostomy tracks.
- **Amplatz Renal Dilation Sets:** Polyurethane radiopaque dilators (8 Fr to 30 Fr) with matching working sheaths for single-step and progressive percutaneous nephrostomy tract creation.

### D. Resection Electrodes & Energy Devices
- **TURP / TURBT Resection Electrodes:**
  - Monopolar and Bipolar cutting loops, roller electrodes, and Collins knife electrodes compatible with major resectoscope sheaths (Storz, Olympus, Wolf).
  - Tungsten alloy cutting loops with high thermal stability and minimal tissue thermal spread.
- **Laser Fibers:** Optical silica fibers (200 µm, 272 µm, 365 µm, 550 µm) compatible with Holmium:YAG (2.1 µm wavelength) and Thulium Fiber Laser (TFL) systems, equipped with custom connector terminations (SMA-905).

## 4. Gastroenterology & Interventional Radiology

### A. ERCP & Biliary Solutions
- **Triple-Lumen Sphincterotomes:** Pre-curved tip with insulated cutting wire for precise biliary sphincterotomy and reduced pancreatitis risk.
- **Biliary Extraction Balloons:** Multiple-size inflation balloons (8.5 mm, 12 mm, 15 mm) for biliary stone extraction.
- **Self-Expanding Metal Stents (SEMS):** Braided nitinol covered and uncovered biliary stents with high radial expansion force and anti-migration flared ends.

### B. Vascular Access & Centesis Drainage
- **Introducer Sheath Kits:** Hemostatic valve, radiopaque tip, and smooth dilator-to-sheath transition for radial and femoral artery cannulation.
- **Pigtail Centesis Catheters:** Polyurethane drainage catheters with multiple side holes for fluid collection, pleural effusion, and abscess drainage.
