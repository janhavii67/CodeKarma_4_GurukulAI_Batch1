## 2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for Endourology

### SOP-ENDO-01: Retrograde Double 'J' Stent Placement
1. **Pre-procedure Prep**: Confirm sterile urine culture or prophylactic IV antibiotic administration (e.g., Cefuroxime 1.5g). Verify stent length and diameter from pre-op CT KUB.
2. **Cystoscopy**: Advance rigid or flexible cystoscope (17–19 Fr) into bladder under local or spinal anesthesia. Inspect ureteral orifices.
3. **Guidewire Cannulation**: Introduce 0.035" Indovasive Zebra guidewire through working channel; cannulate ureteral orifice under direct endoscopic vision. Advance wire under C-arm fluoroscopy until floppy tip curls within renal pelvis.
4. **Stent Preparation**: Flush stent inner lumen with sterile saline to activate hydrophilic coating. Thread stent over guidewire.
5. **Stent Advancement**: Engage Indovasive pusher behind stent. Under fluoroscopic guidance, advance stent over wire until upper pigtail enters renal pelvis.
6. **Upper Coil Deployment**: Withdraw guidewire partially (5–8 cm) while stabilizing pusher; confirm upper coil forms completely within renal pelvis under C-arm.
7. **Lower Coil Deployment**: Maintain pusher position; withdraw remaining guidewire across bladder. The distal pigtail will curl automatically within the bladder lumen.
8. **Verification**: Confirm under fluoroscopy that the proximal coil sits inside renal pelvis without parenchymal perforation, shaft spans ureter smoothly without kinking, and distal coil rests completely within bladder lumen.

### SOP-ENDO-02: PCNL Percutaneous Renal Puncture & Track Dilation
1. **Patient Positioning**: Prone position with chest and pelvic bolsters; pad pressure points meticulously.
2. **Retrograde Ureteric Catheterization**: 5 Fr ureteric catheter placed retrograde via cystoscopy; inject dilute radiopaque contrast (iohexol 50%) under fluoroscopy to opacify calices (retrograde pyelogram).
3. **Caliceal Puncture**: Select target calyx (typically posterior lower or middle pole calyx under biplanar fluoroscopy - 0° vertical and 30° oblique). Introduce 18G IP needle into renal calyx. Confirm entry by removing stylet and observing clear urine / contrast drip.
4. **Guidewire Insertion**: Pass 0.038" J-tip fixed core guidewire through needle into calyx and down through UPJ into ureter (safety wire).
5. **Fascial Incision**: Incise skin and lumbodorsal fascia with #11 blade adjacent to guidewire.
6. **Coaxial Dilation (Dilatron)**: Pass 8 Fr guide rod over wire. Sequentially slide 14 Fr, 20 Fr, and 26 Fr telescopic dilators over guide rod with controlled clockwise-counterclockwise twisting motions under continuous fluoroscopy.
7. **Amplatz Sheath Insertion**: Advance 28 Fr or 30 Fr Amplatz working sheath over final dilator into calyx. Remove internal dilators, leaving Amplatz sheath and safety wire in place.
8. **Nephroscopy & Lithotripsy**: Introduce 24 Fr rigid nephroscope through sheath, perform pneumatic or holmium laser lithotripsy, and extract fragments using Retrivo Perk Basket.
