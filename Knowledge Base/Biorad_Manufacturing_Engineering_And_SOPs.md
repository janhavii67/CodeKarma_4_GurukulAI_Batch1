# Biorad Medisys: Manufacturing Engineering, Precision Machining & Cleanroom SOPs

## 1. Advanced Manufacturing Infrastructure

Biorad Medisys operates advanced, vertically integrated medical manufacturing facilities in Pune and Bangalore/Kolar, adhering to **ISO 13485:2016**, **ISO 9001:2015**, **WHO-GMP**, and **US FDA 21 CFR 820** Quality System Regulations.

### Core Manufacturing Capabilities
- **Multi-Axis CNC Swiss Machining:** High-speed 5-axis and 7-axis CNC machining centers (DMG Mori, Citizen, Star) dedicated to orthopedic joint implants, bone screws, and titanium spinal hardware.
- **Wire Electrical Discharge Machining (Wire EDM):** High-precision cutting of micro-slots, specialized dovetail locking mechanisms, and intricate implant geometries down to ±2 µm tolerances.
- **Automated Robotic Superfinishing:** Multi-axis robotic finishing arms operating micro-abrasive polishing stations to produce mirror articular condyle finishes (Ra < 0.01 µm).
- **Physical Vapor Deposition (PVD) Coating Reactor:** In-house vacuum PVD coating reactors depositing Titanium Niobium Nitride (TiNbN) gold ceramic barriers onto CoCrMo and titanium substrates.
- **Precision Polymer Extrusion & Braiding:** Multi-lumen extrusion lines with laser wall-thickness monitoring for Indovasive ureteral stents, access sheaths, and neurovascular catheters.

## 2. Standard Operating Procedures: Machining & Surface Treatment

### SOP-MCH-101: 5-Axis CNC Milling of Femoral Knee Condyles
1. **Material Receipt & Inspection:** Verify medical-grade Cobalt-Chromium-Molybdenum (CoCrMo alloy conforming to ASTM F75) or Titanium Ti6Al4V ELI (ASTM F136) mill test certificates for chemical composition and microstructure.
2. **Setup & Tool Pre-Setting:** Mount solid carbide / diamond-coated end mills in hydraulic tool holders; calibrate tool lengths and runout using non-contact laser tool setters (runout tolerance < 0.002 mm).
3. **Coolant Verification:** Use chlorine-free, sulfur-free biocompatible synthetic coolant; monitor concentration (7.5% - 8.5%) and pH (9.0 - 9.4) daily.
4. **Machining Execution:** Execute roughing, semi-finishing, and 5-axis simultaneous continuous contouring. Ensure continuous high-pressure coolant flushing to prevent thermal distortion of femoral lugs.
5. **Dimensional CMM Inspection:** First-piece and batch inspection on a Coordinate Measuring Machine (CMM). Verify critical dimensions: distal cut thickness (9.0 mm ± 0.05 mm), AP width (± 0.08 mm), and dovetail groove depth (± 0.02 mm).

## 3. SOP-CLN-202: Multi-Stage Ultrasonic Cleaning & Chemical Passivation

Every implant must undergo a validated aqueous cleaning and passivation cycle prior to cleanroom transfer:

### Cleaning & Passivation Sequence
1. **Stage 1 (Pre-Degreasing):** Aqueous spray de-greasing bath at 55°C ± 3°C with 2% neutral medical surfactant to remove gross particulate and bulk machine coolant.
2. **Stage 2 (Ultrasonic Alkaline Bath):** Submersion in multi-frequency (40 kHz) ultrasonic bath containing 1.5% mild alkaline detergent at 60°C ± 2°C for 15 minutes. Acoustic cavitation dislodges embedded micro-particulates.
3. **Stage 3 (Ultrasonic DI Rinse):** High-frequency (80 kHz) ultrasonic bath in deionized (DI) water (conductivity < 1.0 µS/cm) at 45°C for 10 minutes.
4. **Stage 4 (Chemical Passivation per ASTM A967 & ASTM F86):**
   - Submersion in 20% to 25% (v/v) high-purity nitric acid bath (or 10% citric acid bath) maintained at 50°C ± 3°C for **30 minutes**.
   - Chemical Action: Selectively dissolves free surface iron and base metal contaminants, creating a continuous passive chromium/titanium oxide passivation layer.
5. **Stage 5 (Triple Cascade Reverse Osmosis / DI Rinse):**
   - Rinse 1: RO water rinse (conductivity < 5.0 µS/cm).
   - Rinse 2: Deionized water rinse (conductivity < 1.0 µS/cm).
   - Rinse 3: Final hot DI cascade rinse (conductivity < 0.1 µS/cm, endotoxin level < 0.25 EU/mL).
6. **Stage 6 (HEPA Hot-Air Drying):** Dry inside an ISO Class 5 HEPA laminar-flow clean oven at 75°C ± 5°C for 25 minutes.

## 4. SOP-CR-303: Cleanroom Operations, Environmental Limits & Gowning

### Cleanroom Environmental Limits (ISO Class 7 / Class 10,000)
- **Airflow:** Minimum 30 air changes per hour (ACH); positive differential pressure maintained at **+10 Pa to +15 Pa** relative to outer corridors.
- **Temperature & Humidity:** 20°C ± 2°C; 45% ± 5% Relative Humidity.
- **Airborne Particulates:**
  - Particles >= 0.5 µm: Maximum **352,000 particles/m³** (at-rest).
  - Particles >= 5.0 µm: Maximum **2,930 particles/m³** (at-rest).
- **ISO Class 5 Laminar Flow Stations (Sterile Packaging Workstations):**
  - Continuous vertical laminar air flow velocity: **0.45 m/s ± 20%**.
  - Airborne particulates >= 0.5 µm: Maximum **3,520 particles/m³**.

### Strict Gowning Sequence (SOP-CR-GOWN)
1. Step-over bench transition from non-classified footwear to dedicated cleanroom clogs.
2. Thorough 30-second hand scrubbing with chlorhexidine/alcohol soap; dry with warm filtered air; sanitize with 70% sterile filtered Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA).
3. Don disposable non-shedding bouffant hair cover and 3-ply surgical mask.
4. Step into lint-free continuous polyester cleanroom coverall without allowing garment exterior to touch floor or benches.
5. Don high-calf cleanroom booties with snap fasteners; tuck jumpsuit trousers into booties.
6. Don primary sterile powder-free nitrile gloves; pull cuffs over coverall sleeves.
7. Step through the 15-second multi-nozzle high-velocity air shower chamber before entering the ISO 7 production floor.

## 5. SOP-PKG-404: Sterile Barrier Packaging & Sterilization Validation

### Heat Sealing Validation (ISO 11607)
- **Materials:** Medical-grade DuPont Tyvek 1073B lid sealed to custom thermoformed PETG blister trays.
- **Sealing Window Parameters:**
  - Temperature: **125°C ± 3°C**.
  - Pressure: **4.2 bar ± 0.2 bar**.
  - Dwell Time: **2.5 seconds ± 0.2 seconds**.
- **Destructive & Non-Destructive Packaging Testing:**
  - **Seal Strength (ASTM F88):** 180° tensile peel test with minimum threshold of **1.5 N per 15 mm** seal width.
  - **Bubble Emission Leak Test (ASTM F2096):** Internal pressurization under water to detect pinholes down to 250 µm; zero bubbles permitted.
  - **Dye Penetration Test (ASTM F1929):** Capillary dye migration test verifying continuous, uncompromised seal perimeter.

### Sterilization Release Protocols
1. **Gamma Irradiation (ISO 11137):** Used for Orthovasive joint implants. Minimum absorbed dose **25 kGy** from a Cobalt-60 source, establishing a Sterility Assurance Level (**SAL of 10⁻⁶**). Dosimeter readouts verified against calibration curves for every batch release.
2. **Ethylene Oxide (EtO) Sterilization (ISO 11135):** Used for Indovasive Double J stents and catheter kits. Validated cycle utilizing 100% EtO gas under vacuum, followed by minimum 48 hours of forced heated aeration, ensuring residual ethylene oxide and ethylene chlorohydrin levels comply with ISO 10993-7 (< 4.0 mg/device).
