# Biorad Neurovasive: Neurovascular & Peripheral Interventions Catalog

## 1. Division Profile & Clinical Focus
**Neurovasive** is Biorad Medisys' high-technology clinical division focused on neurovascular, endovascular, and peripheral interventions. Neurovasive products are designed to treat life-threatening cerebral vascular conditions (acute ischemic stroke, intracranial aneurysms, intracranial atherosclerotic disease) and peripheral arterial disease (PAD).

---

## 2. Acute Ischemic Stroke (AIS) Solutions

### Olympia Aspiration System
- **Clinical Indication:** Rapid mechanical thrombectomy for emergency revascularization of large vessel occlusions (LVO) in acute ischemic stroke.
- **System Components:**
  - **Large-Bore Aspiration Catheters:** Constructed with multi-zone transition stiffness — a flexible distal tip to navigate the carotid siphon and middle cerebral artery (M1/M2 segments), combined with a high-torque proximal shaft.
  - **Dedicated Vacuum Aspiration Pump:** High-flow, continuous negative pressure generator calibrated to maintain reliable vacuum force (-29 inHg / -98 kPa) for immediate clot ingestion.
  - **Non-Kinking Tubing & Canister Collection Trap:** Transparent, graduated canisters for visual confirmation of retrieved thrombus.

## 3. Neurovascular Implants and Microcatheters

### A. Intracranial Aneurysm Flow Diverters
- **Implant Concept:** Low-porosity braided tubular micro-mesh stent deployed across the neck of wide-necked and fusiform intracranial aneurysms.
- **Biomechanical Mechanism:** Dissects hemodynamic inflow into the aneurysm sac, promoting stagnation, progressive intra-aneurysmal thrombosis, and neointimal endothelial remodeling across the parent vessel.
- **Materials:** High-tensile cobalt-chromium alloy micro-wires interwoven with radiopaque platinum-tungsten marker strands for continuous fluoroscopic visibility during microcatheter deployment.

### B. Distal Access & Microcatheters
- **Hydrophilic Navigational Catheters:** Ultra-low friction hydrophilic outer coating paired with a PTFE lubricious inner lumen (inner diameter 0.0165" to 0.027") for smooth delivery of micro-coils, stents, and therapeutic embolics.

## 4. Peripheral Interventions

### A. Peripheral Transluminal Angioplasty (PTA) Balloons
- **Indications:** Stenotic or occlusive lesions in iliac, femoral, popliteal, and infrapopliteal arteries (critical limb ischemia).
- **Engineering Highlights:**
  - Non-compliant and semi-compliant multi-layer balloon membranes with high rated burst pressures (RBP up to 24-28 atm).
  - Rapid inflation and deflation cycles minimizing vessel occlusion time.
  - Low crossing profile enabling smooth transit through tight calcified arterial plaques.

### B. Peripheral Stent Systems
- **Self-Expanding Nitinol Stents:** Laser-cut nitinol mesh with segmented cell architecture providing high radial resistive force and crush-recoverability against external mechanical compression in the superficial femoral artery (SFA).
